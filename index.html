The Behind The Billions app

The Behind The Billions app is the publishing tool behind the channel. It takes finished episodes and posts them to TikTok, so creators don't have to re-upload and re-type captions by hand.

The app only posts to a TikTok account after its owner has signed in with TikTok Login Kit and approved the requested permissions, and it only posts content the account owner has chosen and reviewed.

What it does

  • Signs in to the creator's TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0).
  • Uploads a finished, rendered episode (MP4, vertical 9:16) with its caption and hashtags.
  • Publishes it with the Content Posting API, labeled as AI-generated content where applicable.
  • Checks the processing status of each post so the creator knows when it is live.

TikTok permissions used

user.info.basic
Shows which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar) so the creator can confirm the right account.
video.upload
Sends a finished episode to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
video.publish
Posts a finished episode directly to the creator's own profile.

We never read other users' data, never post comments or messages, and never sell or share data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. 1

    Research and script

    Each story is researched from public sources and written as a 45 to 60 second script with a clear hook and ending.

  2. 2

    Produce

    Visuals, narration and subtitles are produced and rendered into a vertical 1080×1920 or 720×1280 video.

  3. 3

    Connect TikTok

    The creator authorizes the app once with TikTok Login Kit. Tokens are stored privately on the creator's own machine.

  4. 4

    Review and publish

    The creator chooses the caption, privacy level and interaction settings, and the app posts the episode through the Content Posting API.

  5. 5

    Confirm

    The app polls TikTok's publish status and reports when the video is live or if TikTok rejected it.
