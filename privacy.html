Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

What this is

"Behind The Billions" is an independent short-video channel (TikTok, YouTube Shorts) that tells true stories about billionaires and public figures whose fortunes collapsed through fraud or scandal. This privacy policy covers the internal publishing tool associated with the channel's TikTok developer app, used solely to automate posting the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account.

Who this app serves

This app is operated by a single individual creator for one purpose only: authenticating and publishing content to the creator's own TikTok account (@behind.the.billio14). It is not a public-facing product, does not have external end users, and does not offer sign-in or any service to third parties.

Data we access

Through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, this app accesses only:

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the connected account (via user.info.basic) — used only to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • The ability to upload and publish video content to the connected account (via video.upload / video.publish).

No data from any other TikTok user or account is ever accessed, collected, or stored. The app does not read, collect, or process viewer comments, follower lists, analytics of other accounts, or any personal data belonging to third parties.

Data storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens for the single connected account are stored securely by the creator and used only to authorize automated video publishing. Video files processed by this tool are the channel's own original content. No personal data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Your rights

The TikTok account owner may revoke this app's access at any time from TikTok's app permissions settings. Doing so immediately stops all further access.
